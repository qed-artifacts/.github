# QED-AI

**Quality Evaluation and Disposition Artifacts Institute** develops open, evidence-governed resources that help accountable users move from evaluation to warranted action.

## Mission

Our mission is to improve how artifacts, claims, systems, practices, situations, complex problems, decisions, and bodies of work are evaluated, matured, documented, contextualized, and responsibly carried forward.

QED-AI pursues that mission by developing a cross-domain quality framework, applying it through public-facing disposition dossiers and decision records, and producing or revising design-science artifacts when the evidence warrants an intervention.

## Public-benefit work

- Open, reusable evaluation and disposition artifacts
- Evidence-governed frameworks and decision records
- Educational resources for accountable stewards, evaluators, educators, researchers, practitioners, reviewers, and affected communities
- Transparent documentation of limitations, warrants, and responsible next steps

QED-AI supplies resources; it does not replace the authority, professional judgment, validation duties, or legal obligations of the people who use them.
